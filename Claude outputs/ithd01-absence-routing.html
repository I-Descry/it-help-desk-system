Ticket Assignment & Absence Routing

What happens to a new IT ticket from the moment it's created, through Manual or Auto assignment, and how the flow reacts when the people involved are out of office.

Manual-mode branch — built & tested Auto-mode branch — built & tested ActingApprover — built & live
Two settings that change this
AssignmentMode

Manual or Auto

A Choice field on Ticket Settings. Switches every new ticket between "send an approval card" and "round-robin the Assistants automatically."

Changed by: Charles, or whoever's covering while he's out — e.g. flipped to Auto before a planned leave.
ActingApprover

Delegate the approval card

A Person field on Ticket Settings. When set, Manual-mode cards go here instead of the Manager — via coalesce(ActingApprover, ManualAssignmentApprover). Left blank the rest of the time.

Changed by: whoever covers Charles's absence — set it to themselves (or the Specialist) when he leaves, cleared when he's back. Whoever's delegated never has to hand it back themselves.
Already handled, no extra logic needed
Nikko is out
Auto never touches him regardless — he's manual-only by design. In Manual mode, the approval card already lists who's currently out, so Charles simply won't tap his button. If he does anyway, the ticket just sits assigned to Nikko until he's back.
Tristan or JP alone is out
The diamond ladder above catches this automatically — whichever Assistant is out gets skipped, and the ticket goes straight to the other one.
An Assistant is assigned something anyway
If someone out of office still receives a ticket (a manual pick, or they return to a backlog), it stays visible in their queue — that's the intended behavior, not a bug to route around.
